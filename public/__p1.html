Smells Like Teen Spirit

Six minutes cut in two days at Sound City, built on a riff its writer thought was too close to Boston to keep.

Cobain called it an attempt to write "the ultimate pop song" while mocking the idea of doing so — a teenage revolution anthem he never meant sincerely.
